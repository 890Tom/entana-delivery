# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-02

## Context

The project is public and will be read by people who were not part of the discussions. Choices made early (framework, data model, hosting) are hard to understand later without their reasoning.

## Decision

Significant architectural decisions are recorded as short Markdown files in `docs/adr`, numbered and never deleted. A decision that changes is superseded by a new record.

## Consequences

Newcomers can see why things are the way they are. Writing a record takes a few minutes per decision.

## Alternatives considered

Keeping decisions only in issues or chat history: not discoverable from the repository.
